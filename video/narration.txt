SpriteSmith is an offline pixel sprite animator and sheet forge. It opens with a real four frame walk cycle, not a mockup.

The editor uses a fixed sixteen slot indexed palette. Slot zero is transparent, and every other color is deliberate, so there is no free color picker hiding the constraint.

Drawing happens on an exact eight, sixteen, or thirty two pixel grid. Onion skin shows the previous and next frames while the timeline plays at the chosen frame rate.

Frames can be duplicated, reordered by selection, repaired with undo, and checked live. The constraint panel explains empty frames, bad palette indexes, fractional origins, and sheet overflow with a recovery hint.

The forge packs the animation into a horizontal strip or a grid using integer pixel math. The sheet preview shows the exact dimensions before anything is exported.

Export creates two machine readable files. One is a true indexed color PNG, and the other is a JSON atlas with frame rectangles and millisecond durations. GIF export is intentionally absent.

SpriteSmith runs with no account, no network service, and no Photoshop integration. Its tests verify the palette, timeline, packing math, atlas shape, PNG bytes, and every chunk checksum.
